# Academic Review & Editorial Skill (Arabic, English, Indonesian)

This skill guides the agent in conducting rigorous, high-standard academic peer reviews and editorial assessments for scholarly articles, book chapters, and educational materials across Arabic, English, and Indonesian.

## 1. Review Framework & Core Evaluation Pillars

When conducting a comprehensive manuscript review, structure the evaluation across these six pillars:

1. **Title, Abstract & Keywords**:
   - Is the title clear, concise, and reflective of the core contribution?
   - Does the abstract succinctly cover: Background, Research Objective/Problem, Methodology, Key Findings, and Scholarly Contribution?
   - Are keywords relevant, standard, and optimized for indexing?

2. **Introduction, Literature Review & Problem Formulation**:
   - Is the research problem clearly stated with a distinct *Research Gap*?
   - Is the theoretical framework appropriate and up to date?
   - Does the author clearly articulate the *Novelty* (kebaruan) compared to previous studies?

3. **Methodology & Rigor**:
   - **Quantitative**: Sampling technique, sample size justification, instrument validity & reliability (Cronbach's alpha, AVE, factor loading), statistical assumptions testing (normality, multicollinearity, homoscedasticity), and choice of statistical model (SEM-PLS, ANOVA, Multiple Regression, etc.).
   - **Qualitative**: Methodological paradigm, data collection techniques (in-depth interview, participant observation, focus group), informant selection (purposive/snowball), data triangulation, and credibility/transferability assessment.
   - **Library / Epistemological / Archival**: Primary vs. secondary source criticism, internal/external textual validity.

4. **Results & In-Depth Discussion**:
   - Are findings presented clearly through proper tables and visualizations without redundancy?
   - Does the discussion critically interpret *why* the findings occurred, linking them back to the literature review rather than merely repeating statistical figures?

5. **Conclusion, Implications & Limitations**:
   - Do conclusions directly answer the research questions without overgeneralization?
   - Are theoretical and practical/policy implications explicitly detailed?
   - Are limitations and directions for future research addressed honestly?

6. **References & Citation Integrity**:
   - Citation consistency (APA 7th, Chicago, Turabian, or Harvard).
   - Recency of literature (typically ≥80% from peer-reviewed journals within the last 5–10 years, unless classical primary sources).
   - Traceability of sources and avoidance of citation stuffing / ghost citations.

## 2. Multilingual & Arabic Classical / Modern Scholarship Handling

When reviewing texts involving **Arabic** (Islamic studies, Middle Eastern studies, Arabic linguistics, education):

1. **Textual Verification & Takhrij**:
   - Verify Quranic verses (Surah and ayah number) and Hadith references (rawi, mukharrij, and degree of authenticity if debated).
   - Differentiate between primary classical texts (*kutub al-turath*) and modern secondary commentaries.
2. **Arabic Grammar, Morpho-Syntax & Terminology**:
   - Ensure accurate vocalization/harakat when quoting ambiguous terms.
   - Check terminological precision (*mu'jam al-musthalahat*) in disciplines such as Usul al-Fiqh, Balaghah, Nahwu, and Falsafah.
3. **Transliteration Standards**:
   - Check adherence to standard academic transliteration systems (e.g., IJMES - International Journal of Middle East Studies, ALA-LC, or SKB 3 Menteri/Kemenag for Indonesian journals). Ensure consistent use of macron (ā, ī, ū) and underdots (ḥ, ṣ, ḍ, ṭ, ẓ).
4. **Indonesian & English Academic Register**:
   - Indonesian: Follow EYD V (Ejaan Yang Disempurnakan Edisi V) and standard academic terminology (KBBI / istilah baku).
   - English: Academic tone (no colloquialisms, objective passive/active balance, cohesive transitions).

## 3. Standard Peer Review Report Output Format

When generating the review report for the user/editor, always format it professionally as follows:

```markdown
# PEER REVIEW REPORT / LAPORAN REVIEW MANUSKRIP

**Judul Manuskrip / Manuscript Title**: [Title]
**Bahasa Pengantar / Language**: [Arabic / English / Indonesian]
**Rekomendasi Akhir / Recommendation**: 
- [ ] Accept without revisions
- [ ] Minor Revisions (Revisi Minor)
- [ ] Major Revisions (Revisi Mayor)
- [ ] Reject / Resubmit to another journal

---

### I. General Assessment & Summary (Evaluasi Umum)
- Brief summary of the paper's core thesis and contribution.
- Key strengths (Kelebihan utama).
- Primary weaknesses (Kelemahan mendasar).

### II. Section-by-Section Critical Comments (Catatan Kritis Tiap Bagian)
1. **Title & Abstract**
2. **Introduction & Research Gap**
3. **Research Methodology**
4. **Results & Discussion**
5. **Conclusion & References**

### III. Detailed Line-by-Line Suggestions (Catatan Baris & Teknis)
- [Halaman X, Paragraf Y / Line Z]: Catatan revisi redaksional, terminologi, atau data.

### IV. Constructive Suggestions for the Author (Saran Konstruktif untuk Penulis)
- Concrete steps the author must take to elevate the paper to publication standards.
```
